LibraryLean Privacy Policy
Last updated: 20 October 2026
Your privacy matters. LibraryLean: Duplicate Photo Finder & HEIC Converter (“LibraryLean”, “the app”) is a Windows desktop app that helps you find duplicate photos and videos and convert HEIC images. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the LibraryLean app and to the LibraryLean page in the Microsoft Store.
In short: LibraryLean works on your own computer. Your photos and videos are scanned, compared, converted and moved on your device. They are not uploaded to us, and we do not see them.
Summary
Your files stay on your device. Scanning, duplicate matching, thumbnails and HEIC conversion all happen locally.
No account is needed. You don’t sign in to LibraryLean and we don’t ask for your name or email to use it.
No ads and no selling of data. We don’t show advertising in the app and we don’t sell or rent personal data.
Purchases are handled by Microsoft. Payment and licence details are processed by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft’s own privacy statement.
You are in control. You choose which folders to scan, what to keep, and what to quarantine or delete.
Personal data we collect
The data involved depends on how you use LibraryLean. We keep collection to the minimum needed for the app to work.
Data we do not collect. We do not collect, upload, copy or view your photos, videos, file names, folder names, metadata (such as dates or GPS location embedded in photos), thumbnails or scan results. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, payment details or precise location.
Data that stays on your device. To work, the app handles the following locally:
Files you choose to scan. Photos and videos in the folders you select, including file paths, sizes, dates and embedded image metadata, which are read to find duplicates and to show previews.
Scan results and thumbnails. Temporary results, comparison data and preview thumbnails created while scanning. These are stored in the app’s local data or cache folder on your device.
Your settings. Preferences such as Light, Dark or System colour mode, sensitivity presets, chosen folders and default actions.
Quarantined files. When you use Quarantine, selected duplicates are moved to a quarantine folder on your device so you can review or restore them. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Converted files. When you use the HEIC converter, the converted images (PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, WEBP, SVG or TIFF) are saved to the location you pick on your device.
Data processed by Microsoft when you get the app. If you download, buy or start a free trial through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes your Microsoft account details, payment information, licence and trial status, and device information to deliver the purchase and confirm your licence. We receive only the information Microsoft makes available to developers, such as aggregated acquisition, usage and crash reports in Partner Center. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how Microsoft handles that data.
Data you choose to send us. If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. Please don’t attach private photos unless we ask and you are comfortable doing so.
How we use personal data
We use the limited data described above to:
Provide the app. Find duplicates, show previews, quarantine or delete files you select, and convert HEIC images.
Verify your licence. Confirm your purchase or free-trial status through the Microsoft Store.
Support you. Respond to emails, troubleshoot problems and fix bugs.
Improve and secure the app. Review Microsoft Store crash and performance reports and user reviews to find and fix issues.
Meet legal obligations and protect our rights where required.
We do not use your photos, videos or their metadata to train AI models, build profiles, or for marketing.
Reasons we disclose personal data
We do not sell or rent personal data. We disclose data only:
With your consent, for example when you send us a support email and ask us to look into an issue.
To Microsoft, which operates the Microsoft Store and processes purchases, trials, updates and crash reports on our behalf and in its own right.
To service providers that help us run the business, such as an email provider for support messages. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
When required by law or to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or ourselves.
In a business transfer, such as a sale of the app, in which case this policy’s protections will continue to apply to the data.
Third-party websites and services linked from the app or its Store page have their own privacy practices, which are not covered by this policy.
How to access and control your personal data
Because LibraryLean keeps your data on your device, you hold most of the controls yourself:
Choose what is scanned. The app only reads folders you select.
Review before acting. Nothing is moved, quarantined or deleted until you confirm.
Restore or delete quarantined files. Open the quarantine folder to restore items or delete them permanently.
Clear app data. Uninstalling LibraryLean from Windows removes the app. You can also delete its local settings and cache folders; your photos and quarantined or converted files are only deleted if you delete them.
Microsoft account data. Manage your purchase history, payment methods and privacy choices through your Microsoft account and the Microsoft privacy dashboard.
For any personal data we hold about you (for example, your support emails), you can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict its use, or to withdraw consent where we rely on it. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner where required by law. See the “How to contact us” section below.
If you are in Australia, you have rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. We honour these rights for all users wherever you live, to the extent they apply to data we hold.
Product-specific details
Duplicate photo and video finder. The app compares files on your device using properties such as file size, content fingerprints and visual similarity. Photo matching can find near-identical images based on your sensitivity setting; video matching finds exact copies only. All of this happens locally and no file content or fingerprint leaves your device.
Quarantine and deletion. Quarantine moves selected files to a folder on your device so they can be restored. Permanent deletion cannot be undone, so the app asks you to confirm before it deletes anything. We never access or back up your files.
HEIC converter. Single and batch conversions are performed locally. Converted images may keep or drop embedded metadata depending on the format; you decide where converted files are saved.
Free trial and licence. The paid app is a one-time purchase with a 7-day free trial offered through the Microsoft Store. Trial and licence status is checked through Microsoft’s Store licensing services.
Updates. App updates are delivered through the Microsoft Store.
Internet access. LibraryLean does not need an internet connection to scan, compare or convert files. 
Cookies and similar technologies
The LibraryLean app does not use cookies, web beacons or advertising identifiers. 
Storage, security and retention
Your photos, videos, scan results, settings and quarantine data are stored only on your device, in locations you choose or in the app’s local data folders. Their security depends in part on your device, so we recommend keeping Windows up to date and using device encryption.
We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and to meet legal and business record-keeping obligations, then delete them. Data held by Microsoft is retained under Microsoft’s own policies.
We take reasonable steps to protect the limited data we hold from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store so that installs are signed and verified by Microsoft.
If we hold your data in or transfer it to another country (for example, through an email provider), we use appropriate safeguards so that it remains protected.
Advertising
LibraryLean contains no advertising and does not share personal data with advertisers. We do not use your photos, videos or files to target ads to you.
Children
LibraryLean is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the app works on files stored on your device, parents and guardians should supervise use on shared family computers. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
Diagnostic data
LibraryLean does not itself collect diagnostic or telemetry data. Microsoft may provide us with aggregated, de-identified crash and performance reports for Store apps, which we use only to fix bugs and improve reliability. Your device’s diagnostic data settings in Windows control what Microsoft collects.
Changes to this policy
We update this policy when our app, practices or laws change. When we post changes, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top. If a change is material, such as a new type of data collection, we will provide prominent notice (for example in the app or on the Store page) before it takes effect. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
